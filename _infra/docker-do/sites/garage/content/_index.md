## What this is

This site documents the design, engineering, permitting, and construction planning for rebuilding the existing garage at 1370 Wilbur Avenue as a lightweight steel superstructure with an upper storage loft and integrated south-facing solar roof.

The working direction is a steel frame constructed **around the existing garage**, preserving the stucco walls and slab. The structure adds nine steel columns on new pier foundations, an upper storage level, a south-facing solar array (~7.7 kW), and a low hipped rear roof cap.

## Key findings so far

- **Steel frame**: beam scheme, 58 pieces, 4.0 tons, 104 fitted joints, 6 distinct sections — see the [frame model](/frame-model/)
- **Solar**: ~5.6–6.0 kW recommended, 35° roof pitch, one 13.5 kWh battery
- **Budget**: $185,000–$325,000 (with GC + contingency)
- **Permitting**: Setbacks at 3 ft (retained garage) and 5 ft (proposed addition); coastal overlay status to be verified
- **Engineering**: Frame verified for gravity and wind at 100 psf loft live (worst utilisation 0.777, drift H/434); connections, base plates and foundations still need a licensed engineer

## Regulatory status

The exact zoning, coastal status, setbacks, height, and parcel controls must be verified for this address. A boundary/topographic survey is the highest-priority collection item. Structural calculations in this report are preliminary unless signed and sealed by the responsible California-licensed engineer.
