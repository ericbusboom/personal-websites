## Status: Preliminary feasibility studies exist

The construction sequence for this project is unusual: a steel frame must be erected *around* an existing garage while preserving the stucco walls and slab. This requires careful temporary works and access planning.

### Key construction phases

1. **Demolition**: Strip existing roof (shingles, felt, sheathing, rafters) while protecting the slab with plywood overlay. Asbestos test required for pre-1990 materials.
2. **Foundation excavation**: Machine-auger 9 pier holes (24″ × 36″) around the existing garage. Hand-dig near slab edges.
3. **Foundation construction**: Sonotube forms, rebar cages, concrete pour, anchor bolt setting. Special inspection required.
4. **Steel erection**: Boom truck + ironworker crew (est. 3–4 days). Field-bolted connections at ~56 joints, field welding (~20 hours).
5. **Roof framing and weatherproofing**: Secondary purlins, metal deck, underlayment, standing-seam roof. This must be weathertight before interior work begins.
6. **Wall assembly and interior**: Framing, insulation, vapor barrier, drywall, interior metal panels.
7. **MEP rough-in**: Electrical, lighting, detectors.
8. **Finishes**: Painting, loft deck, flooring, stair/ladder, doors, windows.
9. **Solar**: PV panel mounting and electrical tie-in (may be phased separately).

### Temporary works

- Slab protection during demolition
- Temporary bracing/shoring during steel erection
- Crane access and neighbor protection
- Site fencing and portable toilet
- Construction power panel

### Inspection milestones

- Foundation excavation and rebar (before concrete)
- Anchor bolt placement
- Steel frame alignment and bolting
- Welding inspection
- Roof underlayment
- Electrical rough-in
- Final building inspection
- Solar/electrical final
