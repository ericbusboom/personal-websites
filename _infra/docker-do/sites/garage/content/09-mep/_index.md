## Status: Planned

The MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) and life safety scope covers everything that makes the building functional and code-compliant beyond the structural frame and envelope.

### Electrical

The electrical scope is partially defined in the solar section. Additional needs:

- **100A subpanel** fed from the existing 200A main service
- **12 GFCI receptacles** distributed around the garage
- **240V/50A EV charger circuit** (conduit to parking area)
- **240V/30A hoist circuit** for the 1,000 lb trolley
- **6 LED high-bay fixtures** for interior lighting
- **2 interconnected smoke/CO detectors**
- **Exterior light** at the loading door

### Mechanical / plumbing

No heating or cooling is currently planned for the garage (unconditioned storage space). Plumbing requirements are minimal:

- No water supply planned
- If a sink or hose bib is desired, this must be coordinated with existing plumbing and the sewer lateral
- Ventilation: natural or mechanical per code (to be determined based on use classification)

### Life safety

| Item | Status |
