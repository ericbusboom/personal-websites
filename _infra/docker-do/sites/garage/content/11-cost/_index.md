## Construction Cost Analysis

**Revision 3 · September 2026**

Long-lead items to order early:

- Custom steel fabrication (6–8 week lead time typical)
- Standing-seam metal roof panels
- Insulated metal interior panels
- Solar panels and battery (supply chain dependent)
- Custom doors and windows
