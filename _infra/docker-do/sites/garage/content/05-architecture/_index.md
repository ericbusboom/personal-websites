## ARCH-006: Combined Floor Plan Study

**Revision 4 · 2026-09-15 · Status: Draft**

The current architectural concept places a steel-frame superstructure over the existing garage, preserving all existing stucco walls and the concrete slab. The design adds nine columns, an upper storage loft, and a south-facing solar roof.

### Key dimensions

| Element | Dimension |
|---|---|
| Building footprint | ~618 sq ft |
| Upper loft deck | 276 sq ft (224″ × 177″) |
| Main roof (30° pitch) | 713 sq ft (282″ S × 214″ N + 282″ × 150″) |
| Hip cap roof | ~131 sq ft |
| Interior metal panels (net) | 665 sq ft |
| Wall cavity area | 778 sq ft |

### Features shown in the plan

- **North loading door**: 8′ × 7′ glass, insulated
- **West slider or French door**: access to side yard
- **Fixed windows**: 3 in the north wall
- **Balcony**: off the upper level, with tempered glass guardrail
- **Trolley/hoist**: 1,000 lb capacity for lifting materials to the loft
- **Open ground-level walkways**: under the new exterior posts
