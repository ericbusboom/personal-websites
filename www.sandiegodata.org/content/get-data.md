The San Diego Regional Data Library provides data downloads for data that is otherwise hard to get, and data services for custom projects.

  * [Data Services](</get-data/data-services/> "Data Services"). Our Data Services are designed to help journalists, nonprofits and government agencies get data-driven answers to important social questions. See our [Data Services ](</get-data/data-services/> "Data Services")page for a complete description of our offerings.
  * [Data Repository.](</dataset/>) Our data repository holds datasets that are not easily available online, or that we've augmented with additional information.
  * [Insights](</get-data/insights/>). Data and analysis of current social issues. 
  * [Data Grant.](</get-data/data-grant/>) $6k of pro-bono data services. 
  * [Knowledge Base.](<http://kb.sandiegodata.org/>) Data analysis tips and techniques. 
  * [Projects](</get-data/projects/>). Current, bast and future data projects.
