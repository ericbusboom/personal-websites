The San Diego Regional Data Library provides data downloads for data that is otherwise hard to get, and data services for custom projects.

  * [Data Services](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/get-data/data-services/> "Data Services"). Our Data Services are designed to help journalists, nonprofits and government agencies get data-driven answers to important social questions. See our [Data Services ](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/get-data/data-services/> "Data Services")page for a complete description of our offerings.
  * [Data Repository.](<http://data.sandiegodata.org>) Our data repository holds datasets that are not easily available online, or that we've augmented with additional information.
  * [Insights](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/get-data/insights/>). Data and analysis of current social issues. 
  * [Data Grant.](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/get-data/data-grant/>) $6k of pro-bono data services. 
  * [Knowledge Base.](<http://kb.sandiegodata.org/>) Data analysis tips and techniques. 
  * [Projects](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/projects/>). Current, bast and future data projects.
