This data is being manually scraped from the [San Diego County Coronavirus update page](<https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/programs/phs/community_epidemiology/dc/2019-nCoV/status.html>). The first tab s the summary data, the second is tab uses the IMPORTHTML to get the most recent data, and the remaining tabs are the whole table for a particular date.
