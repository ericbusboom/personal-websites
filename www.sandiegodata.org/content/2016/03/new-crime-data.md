When we last requested crime data from SANDAG, 3 years ago, it took four months of negotiation to get them to admit they could produce it, and two more months to get the price down to a reasonable amount. Last week when I requested an update, I got one clarification email, then a phone call, and the files were in my inbox a few minutes later. Thanks SANDAG! As a bonus, the data is now geocoded to census block and track, making the files more immediately analyzable. You can find both the old data file and the new one [in an Ambry package on our new data repository](<http://sdrdl.data.civicknowledge.com/>).
