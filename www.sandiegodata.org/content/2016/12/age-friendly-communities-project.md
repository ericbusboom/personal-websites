[At tomorrow night's meeting](<http://meetu.ps/33CBsz>), we'll be kicking off two new data projects. The first is the Health Food Access project, [previously announced](</2016/05/healthy-food-access-data-library-project-2/>), and the second is the Age Friendly Communities project, for which we've [just posted the project page](</get-data/projects/>). In this project we will be collecting data to analyze the capacity and affordability of San Diego’s assisted living industry, considering the anticipated need for these services over the next 30 years. Hope you can join us.
