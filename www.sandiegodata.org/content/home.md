The[ San Diego Regional Data Library ](<http://www.sandiegodata.org>)ensures civic, social, governments and journalists in San Diego county have usable neighborhood level data they need to run their operations and tell their stories, so they can inform and influence grantors, decision makers and the public.

[Visit the Data Library's main website](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/>) for details of our work, projects and ideas.
