_The Student Data Contest is in less than two weeks, so it's time to get your tools ready. If you are a student and want a shot at $2,100 in prizes,[ signup for the contest.](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/projects/sdsu-datathon/> "SDSU Datathon") _ One of the best tools available for quickly visualizing data is Tableau, and best of all, if you don't need to connect to a database, [Tableau Public is free](<http://www.tableau.com/public/>). Tableau allows you to [quickly produce beautiful charts and tables](<http://www.tableau.com/public/gallery/taleof100>), and makes it easy to embed those visualizations on the web. Tableau runs on both Mac and Windows, but while it has a very well designed user interface, it does have its biases; you'll want to spend some time learning how it expects you to build visualizations before the contest. So, [download Tableau Public ](<http://www.tableau.com/public/download>) and spend a bit of time learning how to produce basic visualizations. It will really pay off during the contest, and you'll have a valuable addition to your tool box for future use.
