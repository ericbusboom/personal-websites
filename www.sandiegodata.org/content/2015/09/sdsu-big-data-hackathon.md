## Big Data Hackathon on Oct 3 at 9AM at SDSU!

On October 3, the [Center for Human Dynamics in the Mobile Age](<http://humandynamics.sdsu.edu/Hackathon.html> "HDMA  center organizes the 2015 Big Data Hackathon for San Diego") will host a Big Data Hackathon at San Diego State University. Contestants will use data analysis and programming to solve civic problems related to water conservation, disaster recovery and crime prevention. Visit the [Devpost website for details and to register](<http://bigdataforsandiego.devpost.com/>), or [hit Github for the tech details and data](<http://bigdataforsandiego.github.io/>).
