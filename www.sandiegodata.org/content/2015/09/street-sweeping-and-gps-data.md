Last week the Library worked with Wendy Fry at NBC7 to analyze [GPS records of street sweeping and parking violations](<http://www.nbcsandiego.com/video/#!/on-air/as-seen-on/Street-Sweeping-Parking-Violations-Need-to-Be-Paid--City/329612951>). We haven't got the data online yet -- it's about 3GB, 11M GPS records -- but let us know if you are interested in accessing it. A condensed version of the data [is available as a heatmap of blocks ](<https://www.google.com/fusiontables/DataSource?docid=1L5K04V8xnLEU0U0ZzMuF-pbpNBU8-slCzffTl7NC>)where tickets were issued on days the streets were not swept.
