The Library can help your organization find professional or volunteer data analysts. While we'll be setting up a directory for professionals later this year, our primary focus is connecting volunteers with nonprofits and civic organizations. Why? Volunteer analysts are not only technically skilled and inexpensive, they are also civic-minded citizens, exactly the sort of person who you'd like to have be more aware of your organization and the problems you are trying to solve. Our civic engagement program will conduct two kinds of events: analysis projects and afternoon or weekend data events.
