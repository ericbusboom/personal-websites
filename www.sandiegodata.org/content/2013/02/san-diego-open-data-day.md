This weekend, Saturday Feb 23, is [International Open Data Day](<http://opendataday.org/>), sponsored by the Open Knowledge Foundation, and it is also [Code Across America](<http://brigade.codeforamerica.org/pages/codeacross>) data, sponsored by [Code for America](<http://codeforamerica.org/>). San Diego is participating, hosted by [Open San Diego](<http://opensandiego.org/>), and the Library staff will be there to talk about the Library and our Crime Mapping Project. If you'd like to participate in the conference sessions or hackathon, [visit the EventBrite page to sign up.](<http://codeacross-sandiego.eventbrite.com/>) Hope to see you there.
