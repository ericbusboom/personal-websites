For tonight's meetup [about working with survey data to study inequality](<https://www.meetup.com/San-Diego-Regional-Data-Library/events/275110074/>) we will be working with data from the [Survey of Consumer Finances](<https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm>) in these two Google Colaboratory notebooks: 

  * [Getting Started with the Survey of Consumer Finance](<https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1XGB2KyO8hTcD9BpFKkWLYmDEnJ3lK-fi?usp=sharing>)
  * [Basic SCL Descriptive Statistics](<https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1Whg0dO3briyQTRZgvme75bwQruWYWwpL?usp=sharing>)


The Data Library [also has an extract file](<http://data.sandiegodata.org/dataset/federalreserve-gov-consumer-finances-2016e2019-inherit/>) of the data used in these notebooks, which you can download to replicate the analyses in a different environment.
