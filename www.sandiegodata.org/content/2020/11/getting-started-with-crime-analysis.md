Next Tuesday the Data Library will be hosting a presentation on how to get started with analyzing crime data. 

https://www.meetup.com/San-Diego-Regional-Data-Library/events/274436132/ 

This will be a hands-on presentation, with demonstrations using multiple tools, including: 

  * [Google Colaboratory](<https://colab.research.google.com>)
  * [QGIS](<https://www.qgis.org/en/site/>)
  * [Tableau Public](<https://public.tableau.com/en-us/s/>)


If you want to follow along, click on the links above and QGIS and Tableau. For Google Colaboratory, you can also use Jupyter Notebook, which is usually best to run via [Anaconda](<https://www.anaconda.com/products/individual>). 

Here are links to the data package we will be using, along with the names of the individual datasets:

  * incidents: [San Diego Region Crime Incidents 2007 – 2013](</dataset/sandag-gov-crime-2007e2013/>)

  * ca_abc_licenses: [California and San Diego Alcohol Licenses](</dataset/abc-ca-gov-licenses/>)
  * sd_abc_density: [California and San Diego Alcohol Licenses](</dataset/abc-ca-gov-licenses/>)
  * tracts: [San Diego Demographics By Tract](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-demographics-tract/>)
  * age_sex: [San Diego Demographics By Tract](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-demographics-tract/>)
  * total_population [San Diego Demographics By Tract](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-demographics-tract/>)
  * raceeth: [San Diego Demographics By Tract](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-demographics-tract/>)
  * pd_beats: [San Diego Police Regions and Demographics](</dataset/sandiego-gov-police-regions/>)
  * pd_divisions: [San Diego Police Regions and Demographics](</dataset/sandiego-gov-police-regions/>)
  * pd_neighborhoods: [San Diego Police Regions and Demographics](</dataset/sandiego-gov-police-regions/>)
