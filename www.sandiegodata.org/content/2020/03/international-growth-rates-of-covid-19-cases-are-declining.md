Similar to the previous post about [case growth rates in US states](</2020/03/hopeful-trends-in-covid-19-growth-rates/>), growth rate are declining in most countries. 

![](https://i1.wp.com/www.sandiegodata.org/uploads/7/2020/03/Screen-Shot-2020-03-26-at-11.35.37-AM.png?fit=817%2C1024&ssl=1)

The global average for the decline in growth rates is about .9% per day. Extrapolating this decline would result in countries hitting zero new cases per day at about 40 days from when then hit 100 cases. China had a growth rate of .7% ad day 40, and less than .1% at day 52. A more accurate prediction model would be non linear, which is had to see in most of these plots, except for South Korea, which clearly shows the approach to zero.
