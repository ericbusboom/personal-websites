We've updated our crime data collection with files for 2016 and 2017. Our crime data collection now runs from 2006 to 2017, with two major files: 

  * [San Diego Region Crime Incidents 2007 - 2013](<http://data.sandiegodata.org/dataset/clarinova_com-crime-incidents-casnd-7ba4-extract>)
  * [Raw San Diego County Crime Incidents 2012-2017](<http://data.sandiegodata.org/dataset/raw-san-diego-county-crime-incidents-2012-2015>)
