Over the past few months, the Data Library has been involved in several projects to study homelessness in San Diego. The main projects have been:

  * [Digitizing 5 years of monthly homelessness counts](<https://downtown-homelesness.sandiegodata.org/>) for Downtown San Diego.
  * Building a [collection of data about homeless issues ](<http://data.sandiegodata.org/collections/homelessness/>)for San Diego and Los Angeles.


Last Spring, we got the first portion of the Downtown homelessness data converted with the [help of Kearney High students](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/2019/06/homeless-in-the-gaslamp-district/>), and this fall, we'll be [finishing the rest of the data conversion](<https://downtown-homelesness.sandiegodata.org/>) and doing data analysis. If you'd like to participate, [you can contact me](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/about/contact/>), or watch for our [next project meetup](<https://www.meetup.com/San-Diego-Regional-Data-Library/>). 

For examples of the work we'll be doing on this project, visit the [Civic Knowledge Insights blog](<https://insights.civicknowledge.com/?s=homeless>), or visit our [data collection for the core datasets](<http://data.sandiegodata.org/collections/homelessness/>), or check out the [downtown homeless digitization project.](<http://downtown-homelesness.sandiegodata.org/>)

But the best examples of the future work is the past work of last year's Kearny High students, such as this [analysis of homelessness clusters ](</uploads/7/2019/08/Homeless-Analysis-Sean-and-Kasey.pdf>) that identified one of the largest clusters as being the result of the Gaslamp 15 theater shutting down. We hope for more of this sort of student analysis next year.
