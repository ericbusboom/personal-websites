Over the summer we've been working on [a project to digitize maps of homeless person counts ](<http://downtown-homelessness.sandiegodata.org/>)that have been collected over the last 5 years by the [Downtown San Diego Partnership](<https://downtownsandiego.org/>). We now have partial results for three neighborhoods, with data positions shown in the map below. 

We expect to have the dataset complete in the next few weeks, and after some additional quality assurance, it will be ready for analysis. If you are researcher, part-time analysts, or concerned citizen who wants to use a unique datasets of 5 years of monthly position reports for homeless individuals, [let us know. ](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/about/contact/>)
