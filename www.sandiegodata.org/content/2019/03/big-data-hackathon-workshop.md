For this workshop, you will need to install: 

  * [QGIS](<https://qgis.org>). Also, install OpenLayers [Tableau](<https://public.tableau.com/en-us/s/>)

Review of Data Sources 
  * Data Lists for the [Hackathon](<http://bigdataforsandiego.github.io/>)
  * [Census Reporter](<https://censusreporter.org/>)
  * [Healthy Places Index](<https://healthyplacesindex.org/>)
  * [Sangis](<http://www.sangis.org/>)
  * [Planning Database](<http://planning.sandiegodata.org/>)

Major Concepts 
  * Census, Tracts and Zip Codes
  * Joins: Attribute and Spatial

Datasets to load 
  * Census Reporter. [Median Household Income in the Past 12 Months](<https://censusreporter.org/data/table/?table=B19013&geo_ids=05000US06073,140|05000US06073&primary_geo_id=05000US06073>)
  * [San Diego Tracts and Communities](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-geography-2018/>)
  * [Healthy Places Index](</dataset/healthyplacesindex-org-healthy-places-index-san-diego/>)
  * [County Planning Database](</dataset/sandiegodata-org-planning-tracts/>)
  * [San Diego Crime Incidents](</dataset/>)
  * [Alcohol Permits](</dataset/>)
  * [SANGIS Business Sites](</dataset/sangis-org-business-sites/>)
  * 
Agenda 
  * Loading a QGIS basemap
  * Census via Census Reporter
  * Tracts, basic styling, boundaries, alpha.
  * Attribute joins.
  * Median Income in Tableau

Tips 
  * Avoid Zip Codes ( Show a map )
  * Breaks. Jenks and Quantile
