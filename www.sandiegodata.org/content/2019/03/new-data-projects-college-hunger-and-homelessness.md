We will soon be getting started with three new data projects: 

  * [San Diego Hunger Coalition](<https://www.sandiegohungercoalition.org/>): build analysis datasets for Universal Free Meals and Breakfast Before the Bell programs.
  * [Reality Changers](<https://realitychangers.or>): Analyze and identify improvements for the Challenge Assemblies program.
  * [San Diego Downtown Partnership](<https://downtownsandiego.org/>): digitize and analyze monthly homeless count data.

For the [San Diego Hunger Coalition](<https://www.sandiegohungercoalition.org/>), we will be collecting a lot of datasets, combining them into a package, and supporting the SDHC staff in data analysis. The project will involve a lot of data sleuthing and wranging. For [Reality Changers](<https://realitychangers.or>), we will be building an analysis data package form a collection of Google spreadsheets, then analysing student performance. For [San Diego Downtown Partnership ](<https://downtownsandiego.org/>)we will be digitizing 5 years of monthly homelessness count maps, then analyzing the changes in downtown homelessness and the relationship between homelessness presence and land use. If you'd like to join one of these projects, be sure to join one of our announcement channels: our Meetup group, the mailing list, Facebook, or Twitter, [all linked from our Contact page. ](<http://www.sandiegodata.org/about/contact/>)I've got a bit more project initiation tasks, then we will send out complete announcements for each project, later this week.
