Volunteer analysts that need help with specific parts of a Data Library pro-bono project can attend Office Hours to get help with installing software, learning Python, Pandas or Jupyter, or any other of the skills and tasks involved in a data project. To attend Office Hours, select the "Office Hour" event below and select a date and time.
