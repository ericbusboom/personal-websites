**The San Diego Regional Data Library works with nonprofits, governments and journalists to answer data driven questions**

## [Data Insights](<https://insights.sandiegodata.org>)

Our[ Data Insights Service](<https://insights.sandiegodata.org>) is a question and answer forum for data driven insights about San Diego area demographics, environment business and public safety issues. [Ask a question](<https://insights.sandiegodata.org/qa>) for our analysts to solve, or contribute what you know with an answer.

## [Data Repository](</dataset/>)

The Data Repository holds data packages for a wide variety of topics, and larger collections for Education and Urban Planning
