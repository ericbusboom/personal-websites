If your organization has questions that can be answered with data, you can have an undergraduate data analyst work on your project, with professional guidance, for up to 12 weeks.  Project sponsors must be able to describe their needs as a set of questions that can be answered with available data, and must be able to meet with the interns at their site for 1 hour per week. Projects must have a social goal or benefit.  [We're currently running two projects](</get-data/projects/>), and expect to be running three or four more this summer. If your organization needs data analysis this summer, please contact the Data Library Director, Eric Busboom at (619) 363-2607 or [eric@sandiegodata.org](<mailto:eric@sandiegodata.org>).
