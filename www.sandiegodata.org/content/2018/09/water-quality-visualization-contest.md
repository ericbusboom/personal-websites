The San Francisco Estuary Institute is running a visualization contest for water quality measurements from San Francisco Bay. The Data Library will be organizing a team to create one or more submissions. If you'd like to join the project,[ come to our next meetup](<https://www.meetup.com/San-Diego-Regional-Data-Library/events/252705680/>) or join our [Water Project Google Group](<https://groups.google.com/a/sandiegodata.org/d/forum/water>). ![](https://www.sfei.org/sites/default/files/RMP_Data_Visualization_Challenge_Flyer.png)
