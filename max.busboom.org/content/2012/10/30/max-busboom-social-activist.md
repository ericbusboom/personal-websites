![donttouch](/uploads/10/2012/10/donttouch-295x300.jpg)Max came home from school excited that he "used the power of words to save the caterpillars." He was concerned that other kids were molesting the caterpillars, so he formed the "Caterpillars Team," a group of students and one teacher who would protect the caterpillars. He spent the afternoon lecturing us about how sad it would be if the caterpillar population were wiped out. Then he made some fliers for me to copy and distribute. If you'd like to help, you can download the [PDF file of the Save The Caterpillars](<https://dl.dropbox.com/u/39949226/Max/SaveTheCaterpilliars.pdf>) flyer and pass it around.
