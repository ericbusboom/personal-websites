The periods of the [geologic time scale](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geologic_time_scale> "Geologic time scale") are fascinating, it seems, and Max enjoys relating facts about them, although this observation, in reference to a picture illustrating some of the important periods, was also a bit poetic: 

> He's walking to the Quaternary
