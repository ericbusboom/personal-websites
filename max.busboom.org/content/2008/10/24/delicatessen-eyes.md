[![delicatesseneyes](/uploads/10/2008/10/delicatesseneyes-300x226.jpg)](</uploads/10/2008/10/delicatesseneyes.jpg>) Max invents a lot of phrases, and many of them are mysteries to his parents for months. This one took some sleuthing to define: 

> Delicatessen Eyes: The eyes of a person whose lower face is hidden.

Max came up with names for all of the [James Barnett](<http://jamesbarnett.net/>) Hats paintings that we have. He calls the [Fez painting](<http://www.flickr.com/photos/elreyart/459047711>) "Sabbi-Student" and the Biretta painting is "Obbi-Student". I told the artist about Max's names, and he replied: 

> Oh, that is BEAUTIFUL. I guess the counter at the deli, to a 4 YO, is pretty high, so they only see the clerk's eyes? That makes me happy. Thank you for sharing!
